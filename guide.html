Battery safety

Vape batteries hold a lot of energy. A few simple habits keep them, and you, safe.

  • Use the right charger. Charge with the cable or charger made for your device, from a normal USB port or plug. Avoid cheap fast chargers.
  • Don't charge and forget. Unplug when it's full, and don't leave it charging overnight or on a bed or couch.
  • Never carry loose batteries in a pocket. Keys and coins can short them out. Use a plastic battery case.
  • Check the wrap. If the plastic sleeve on a removable battery is torn or nicked, stop using it until it's rewrapped or replaced.
  • Stop if something's off. A battery or device that's swollen, leaking, very hot or dropped in water should not be used. Bring it in and we'll take a look.
  • Keep it out of the heat. No car dashboards in the sun. Recycle old batteries at a drop-off point, not in the bin.
02

When to replace coils

Every coil wears out. How fast depends on how much you vape, your power setting and how sweet your juice is.

  • It tastes burnt or dry. The clearest sign. Change it now, as a burnt coil won't recover.
  • The flavour has faded. If your favourite juice tastes flat or muddy, the coil is clogged.
  • Gurgling or leaking. A tired coil can flood. If a clean and refill doesn't fix it, swap it.
  • Less vapour than before. Weaker clouds at the same setting usually mean a worn coil.
  • Prime every new coil. Drip a few drops on the cotton, fill the tank and wait 5 to 10 minutes before your first puff. Start at the low end of the wattage printed on the coil.
  • Not sure which coil fits? Use the Coil & Pod Finder, or WhatsApp us a photo of your device.
03

Choosing your strength

The right nicotine strength depends on your device and how much you used to smoke. Too strong feels harsh; too weak leaves you wanting more.

  • Freebase e-liquid is the classic juice for tanks and bigger clouds. It comes in low strengths like 3mg and 6mg.
  • Nic salts are smoother at higher strengths, made for pod kits and mouth-to-lung (MTL) devices with a tighter draw.
  • Disposables are pre-filled, usually with nic salts. The box shows the strength, often as a percentage (2% is 20mg/ml, 5% is 50mg/ml).
0 – 6mgFreebase, sub-ohm tanks and big clouds. Light or social smokers.
10 – 20mgNic salts in pod kits and MTL. A good start for most smokers switching over.
20mg +Nic salts and many disposables. Heavier smokers. Go lower if it feels harsh.

Feeling dizzy, getting headaches or a harsh throat? Step down a strength. Still craving cigarettes? Step up one. Ask us and we'll help you find your spot.

04

Good habits

Responsible vaping protects your gear and the people around you.

  • Adults only. Vapes contain nicotine, which is addictive. They're not for anyone under 18, non-smokers, or anyone pregnant or breastfeeding.
  • Store juice safely. Keep e-liquid out of the sun and well away from children and pets. Nicotine is poisonous if swallowed.
  • Keep it clean. Wipe the mouthpiece and connections now and then, and never let a tank run completely dry.
