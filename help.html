Delivery & help

How ordering works, how you get it, and straight answers about delivery and returns.

How to order

Three steps

Fill your bag

Browse the shop and add what you want. Pick a flavour or colour where there's a choice.

Send it on WhatsApp

Open your bag, choose how you want it, and tap send. Your order arrives as a message to us on 074 640 6614.

We confirm, you pay

We check stock, confirm the total (and any delivery cost) and tell you how to pay. Then it's yours.

Getting it to you

Ways to get it

Collect in store

Pick it up at 231 Monument Road, Glen Marais once we've told you it's ready.

  • No delivery fee
  • Bring your ID
  • Choose "Collect" in your bag

Courier across SA

We ship anywhere in South Africa. The cost is worked out from your address and the size and weight of your order, and we tell you before you pay.

  • Dispatched in 3 to 5 business days
  • Tracking number by email
  • ID checked on delivery (18+)

7Waves Soon

A delivery service built only for vape shops, launching on the East Rand. When it reaches Glen Marais, a rider brings your order from our counter.

  • Shop prices, plus a delivery fee for the rider
  • ID and a 4-digit code checked at your door
  • See 7Waves
FAQs

Good questions

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp, call 074 640 6614 or email info@mmffusion.com.

Do you deliver outside Kempton Park?

Yes, anywhere in South Africa. Delivery is charged by your address and the size of the parcel, and we confirm it on WhatsApp before you pay.

How long does delivery take?

We aim to dispatch within 3 to 5 business days of confirming your order, then the courier takes over. Public holidays and courier delays can add time. Once it's sent, we email you a tracking number.

What if a delivery fails?

If the courier can't deliver because the address is wrong or no one is home, we'll contact you to arrange another try. Repeat deliveries or address changes may cost extra.

Can I order online and collect?

Yes. Choose "Collect" in your bag and send your order. We'll let you know on WhatsApp when it's packed and waiting at 231 Monument Road.

How do I pay?

Send your order on WhatsApp and we'll confirm the total and how to pay before anything is charged. Not sure? Just ask when you message us.

Can I return something?

Yes, within 7 days of receiving it, as long as it's unused, unopened and in its original packaging. E-liquids can only come back sealed, for hygiene reasons. Email info@mmffusion.com with your order details and the reason, and we'll explain the next step. Return postage is yours unless the item is faulty or wrong.

What if it arrives damaged or faulty?

Tell us within 48 hours of receiving it, with your order details and a photo if you can. Faulty or incorrect items are the exception to the return-postage rule.

When will I get my refund?

Once we've received and checked your return, we let you know if it's approved, then refund to your original payment method within 14 business days. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.

Can I swap for something else?

We don't do exchanges. Return the item for a refund and order the one you want.

What can't be refunded?

Opened e-liquids, and clearance or promotional items where stated, unless they're faulty or arrived damaged.

What is 7Waves?

7Waves is a delivery service built only for vape shops, launching on the East Rand. It isn't live for us yet. When it is, you'll pay shop prices plus a delivery fee that goes to the rider, who checks your ID and a 4-digit code before handing over your order.

Do I need to be 18?

Yes. We only sell to adults and check ID in the shop, on collection and on delivery.

Which coil or pod fits my device?

Use our Coil & Pod Finder: pick your device and it shows every coil, pod and tank that fits. Still not sure? Send us a photo of your device on WhatsApp.
